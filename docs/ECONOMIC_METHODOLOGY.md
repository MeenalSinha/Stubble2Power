## 1. Techno-Economic Principles & Integrity

A primary flaw in many clean-tech proposals is projecting financial viability solely by assuming high carbon offset credit sales. In carbon markets, verification, validation, and issuance cycles take $12\text{--}24\text{ months}$ and involve significant discounting.

**Stubble2Power Core Rule:** Operational financial viability MUST be demonstrated based entirely on **physical product sales** (densified briquettes and biochar) before any speculative carbon credits are considered. Carbon credit additionality is computed separately as an environmental milestone, not as operating working capital.

## 2. Capital Budgeting & Operating Expense Equations

### A. Capital Expenditure (CAPEX)
$$\text{Total CAPEX} = N_{\text{units}} \times \text{CAPEX}_{\text{unit}}$$
Where:
- Nominal Mobile Densifier CAPEX: $\text{INR } 2,500,000$ ($25\text{ Lakhs}$) per trailer-mounted unit.
- Straight-line economic useful life: $7.0\text{ years}$ (Companies Act Schedule II standard).
- Annual Straight-Line Depreciation:
  $$\text{Depreciation} = \frac{\text{Total CAPEX}}{7.0}$$

### B. Gross Operating Revenue (Physical Sales)
$$\text{Total Revenue} = (\text{Briquette Tons} \times P_{\text{briquette}}) + (\text{Biochar Tons} \times P_{\text{biochar}})$$
- Nominal Base Case Prices:
  - Briquette Market Price: $\text{INR } 6,000\text{/ton}$ ($\text{INR } 6.0\text{/kg}$).
  - Biochar Market Price: $\text{INR } 15,000\text{/ton}$ ($\text{INR } 15.0\text{/kg}$).

### C. Operating Expenses (OPEX)
$$\text{Total OPEX} = \text{Farmer Payments} + \text{Logistics Transport} + \text{Processing Fuel \& Labor}$$
Where:
- **Farmer Direct Incentive Payment:** $\text{INR } 1,500\text{/ton}$ collected ex-field.
- **Logistics Transport Cost:** Road route diesel consumption $+$ driver operational overhead.
- **Processing Fuel & Consumables:** Auxiliary generator diesel ($4.5\text{ L/ton} \times \text{INR } 89.50\text{/L} = \text{INR } 402.75\text{/ton}$) $+$ field operator labor and press die wear ($\text{INR } 450.0\text{/ton}$).

## 3. Financial Return Indicators

### Break-Even Tonnage
$$\text{Contribution Margin / Ton} = \frac{\text{Total Revenue} - \text{Total OPEX}}{\text{Biomass Tons}}$$
$$\text{Annual Fixed Costs} = \text{Annual Depreciation} + (5\% \times \text{Total CAPEX Overhead})$$
$$\text{Break-Even Biomass (Tons)} = \frac{\text{Annual Fixed Costs}}{\text{Contribution Margin / Ton}}$$

### Payback Period & Return on Investment (ROI)
$$\text{Annual Operating Profit (EBITDA)} = (\text{Gross Profit} \times 2.0) - \text{Annual Fixed Costs}$$
$$\text{Simple Payback Period (Years)} = \frac{\text{Total CAPEX}}{\text{Annual Operating Profit}}$$
$$\text{ROI \%} = \frac{\text{Annual Operating Profit}}{\text{Total CAPEX}} \times 100\%$$

### Discounted Cash Flow (NPV & IRR)
Using India clean-energy benchmark Weighted Average Cost of Capital ($\text{WACC} = 10.5\%$):
$$\text{NPV} = -\text{Total CAPEX} + \sum_{t=1}^{7} \frac{\text{Operating Cash Flow}_t}{(1 + \text{WACC})^t}$$
Where $\text{Operating Cash Flow}_t = \text{Annual Operating Profit} + \text{Annual Depreciation}$.

## 4. Sensitivity Tornado Analysis

The model evaluates elasticity across key price and operational parameters with $\pm 10\%$ and $\pm 20\%$ variations:
1. **Briquette Sale Price Elasticity:** High positive sensitivity ($+1.8$ elasticity).
2. **Biochar Sale Price Elasticity:** Moderate positive sensitivity ($+0.8$ elasticity).
3. **Farmer Procurement Price Elasticity:** Negative sensitivity ($-0.9$ elasticity).
4. **Diesel Fuel Price Elasticity:** Low-to-moderate negative sensitivity ($-0.3$ elasticity).
