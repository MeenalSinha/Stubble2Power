# Agricultural & Land-Use Reference Datasets

## 1. Overview & Provenance
Stubble2Power connects satellite Earth observation hotspots with authoritative agricultural land-use and crop cultivation datasets.
To guarantee scientific integrity (Non-Negotiable Rule 1 & Rule 2: Nothing Hardcoded & No Fake Data), all geospatial references, crop calendars, and land-cover maps maintain verified institutional provenance.

---

## 2. Primary Source Datasets

### 2.1 Land Use / Land Cover (LULC)
- **Dataset**: ESA WorldCover 10m Global Land Cover (v200)
  - **Custodian**: European Space Agency (ESA) & VITO Remote Sensing
  - **Spatial Resolution**: 10 meters
  - **Temporal Baseline**: Annual global composite (2020–2021)
  - **Classification Standard**: UN FAO Land Cover Classification System (LCCS)
  - **Cropland Class**: Value 40 (Cultivated land, herbaceous cropland, flooded crops)
  - **Accuracy**: Over 85% regional overall accuracy for agricultural floodplains in South Asia.
  - **Access URL**: https://esa-worldcover.org

- **Dataset**: NRSC Bhuvan National Land Use / Land Cover (LULC 50k)
  - **Custodian**: National Remote Sensing Centre (NRSC), Indian Space Research Organisation (ISRO)
  - **Spatial Resolution**: 1:50,000 scale (~24m)
  - **Coverage**: Multi-temporal Rabi and Kharif agricultural monitoring across Punjab and Haryana.
  - **Access URL**: https://bhuvan.nrsc.gov.in

### 2.2 Agricultural Crop Calendars & Agronomy
- **Institution**: Punjab Agricultural University (PAU), Ludhiana
  - **Publication**: "Package of Practices for Kharif and Rabi Crops of Punjab" (2024 edition)
  - **Application**: Sowing, harvesting, and residue combustion susceptibility windows for paddy, basmati, wheat, and cotton.
  - **Access URL**: https://pau.edu

- **Institution**: Indian Council of Agricultural Research (ICAR) & ICAR-CSSRI, Karnal
  - **Publication**: "Crop Residue Management Strategies and National Bio-Energy Protocols" (2024)
  - **Application**: Crop-specific Residue-to-Product Ratios (RPR), sustainable field retention requirements, and collection efficiency benchmarks.
  - **Access URL**: https://cssri.res.in

- **Institution**: Directorate of Economics & Statistics, Ministry of Agriculture & Farmers Welfare (MoA&FW)
  - **Dataset**: Annual Area and Production Statistics for Major Crops in India
  - **Application**: District-level historical grain and residue yields.
  - **Access URL**: https://desagri.gov.in

## 3. Data Governance & Licensing
All datasets are governed by public scientific open-access licenses (Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 or Government of India National Data Sharing and Accessibility Policy - NDSAP).
No private or restricted personal farmer data is ingested from external public sources.
